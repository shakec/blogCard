About

Remy LeBeau has always straddled the line between his past and present life. Raised in the Thieves Guild of New Orleans, the Cajun was trained from childhood to be a perfect thief – illusive and charming. With the ability to charge objects with explosive energy, Gambit can turn a deck of cards into a loaded weapon. As an X-Man, he seeks redemption for sins past by playing an unlikely role – hero.

His tactical mind is shaped less by formal military training and more by a lifetime of high-stakes survival, street-level criminal enterprise, and professional thievery. Approaching combat like a high-stakes poker game he is a master of the "long con" in battle, using his loud, explosive powers and flamboyant personality as a smoke screen to hide his true intentions. Due to this, Remy leads his enemies to underestimating him as reckless. Intentionally leaning into this stereotype he quietly sets up the trap for the win.
